## What this means for the data

You are free to:

- **Share**: copy and redistribute the data in any medium or format.
- **Adapt**: remix, transform, and build upon the data.

Under the following terms:

- **Attribution**: you must give appropriate credit to RaceDex, provide a link to the license, and indicate if changes were made.
- **NonCommercial**: you may not use the data for commercial purposes.
- **ShareAlike**: if you remix, transform, or build upon the data, you must distribute your contributions under the same license.

pages, historical archives, open datasets such as the Ergast database, etc.).
We do not claim ownership over facts such as race results or lap times; the
license above applies to our compilation, structure, corrections and
additions. Each source may have its own terms, and it is the responsibility of
the user to check them when reusing the data. If you are a rights holder and
think something here should be attributed or removed, please open an issue.
